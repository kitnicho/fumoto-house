Fumoto

A mountain house with a story.

Fumoto means “the foot of the mountain” — a short walk from the Romance Chair and the slopes of Myoko Suginohara.

Ski to the door. Four bedrooms, a tatami cinema, a generous kitchen and the kind of character that only comes from a house made slowly, personally and with care.

01 · The house

Traditional textures, honest materials and thoughtful renovation — made for long snow days and slow evenings together.

A proper mountain home, not a purpose-built rental.

Fumoto sits in Suginosawa, away from the busier resort centre. Inside, old timber, tatami and shoji meet a generous social kitchen, comfortable beds and the practical details a ski house actually needs.

The result is warm without being rustic, considered without being precious — and deliberately different from the more functional lodges around it.

A house built with care. Carried forward with care.

Fumoto has a history before it has a brand. The house was shaped by hand, piece by piece, as a mountain home — and that personal attention remains visible in the timber, the objects, the rooms and the way people gather here.

Today Akiko continues that spirit: warm, generous and quietly attentive. The story belongs to the house, but guests become part of it for a few days at a time.
